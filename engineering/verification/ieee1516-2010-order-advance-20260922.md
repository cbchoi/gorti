# Preferred Order And Advance Eligibility

Date: 2026-09-22
Status: Implemented; bounded Windows/Linux regression verification complete.

This bounded W1 change follows the [RO queue work](ieee1516-2010-ro-queue-20260922.md).
It does not complete all time-management services or IEEE 1516-2010 conformance.

## Source And Scope

The supplied IEEE 1516.1-2010 redline is the behavioral oracle. The active 2010
text in 8.1.2 (PDF 182-184) defines sent and received order independently; the
presence of a timestamp alone does not establish TSO. Sections 8.1.3, 8.1.5-8.1.7
(PDF 184-189) distinguish join, logical-time advancement, regulation, constraint
and delivery eligibility. Sections 8.10-8.11 (PDF 203-205) supply the next-message
target and strict/Available boundaries. Licensed sources remain outside Git.

The batch covers three related corrections:

- Apply FOM preferred order in every runtime profile, with explicit instance or
  interaction overrides taking precedence. Mixed attribute updates retain their
  separate order groups, and deletion follows the privilege attribute's order.
- Permit finite time advances by joined federates without either time mode.
  An unconstrained requester, including a regulating-only requester, must not
  wait for a stationary peer's GALT. Membership validation remains at the real
  service boundary rather than being approximated by time-mode flags.
- Hold constrained TSO while time-granted even when its timestamp equals the
  current logical time and asynchronous RO delivery is enabled. NMR/NMRA include
  a queued equal-time message when selecting the next grant target.

changes are introduced by this batch. Domain logic remains independent of
transport and persistence encoding.

## Remaining Work

- Full FQR behavior: flush all queued TSO, choose the required minimum grant
  target and support a same-time grant when no forward progress is possible.
- Safe regulation/constraint engagement times, including late engagement after
  an unengaged advance. Preserving logical time is not proof of that protocol.
- Full delayed subscription evaluation, including send-time unsubscribed
  candidates, and send-time DDM region extents.
- Complete Retract preconditions, including the strict sender current/requested
  time plus actual-lookahead bound, and Request Retraction for already-delivered
  messages, including received-RO and FQR deliveries (8.21-8.22, PDF 215-217).
  Undelivered messages must be dequeued. Notification absence is not an oracle:
  8.22 does not require proof of prior delivery before a notification. Callback
  designator metadata and internal queue removal alone do not establish these
  services.
- Reject or otherwise handle corrupt past-TSO state without delivering below
  current logical time. Timestamped RO below current time is a different case.
- Exact standardized time representations and the legacy `+Inf` sentinel.
- Complete normative inventory, independent execution evidence, release
  identity and end-to-end MOM callback accounting.
