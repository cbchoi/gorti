# SRS Historical Summary: v3 Research and Reach

Status: Historical summary; non-normative

Reconstructed: 2026-07-17 from repository history and surviving specification
content; not a release date

Context: Summarizes the documented v3 Research and Reach stage

Authority: The [current specifications](../../current/) define the formal
engineering baseline.

This cumulative historical summary describes the documented v1 and v2 scope
together with a C++ federate SDK, typed Python handles and collections, wider
callback-model support, complete cross-process service exposure, structured
save snapshots, mTLS/OIDC options, administrative observability, and extension
points for alternative algorithms and transports.

Research alternatives were required to remain opt-in, deterministic, and
semantically equivalent to the default implementation. Experimental cluster
work did not become a supported production deployment claim.
